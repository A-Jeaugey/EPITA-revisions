Based on the 7 midterm 1 papers found, from November 2017 to October 2022. The archive has none for 2023 to 2025.

Know this before you revise

The 7 old papers are only about kinematics (Cartesian and polar coordinates). Forces, Newton’s second law and energy are on this year’s syllabus but came up in none of them: nobody knows yet how they will be tested. For these chapters, the only models are TD 2, 3 and 4. Do not leave them aside.

Polar coordinates are the opposite case: they came up in every old midterm, but this year they come after energy (TD 5), so it is not sure they will be in midterm 1. Revise them if you have time or if your teacher says so.

Planning

A plan in six sessions

About 1 h 30 per session, in order. Add ten minutes of MCQ every day.

  1. The three vectors. Chapter 1, parts 1 to 5, then methods 1 to 3. Exercises: TD 1 Ex 1 and TD 1 Ex 4.
  2. Integrate and launch. Chapter 1, parts 6 to 8, then methods 4 to 6. Exercises: TD 1 Ex 3, then the projectile of the November 2021 midterm.
  3. Forces and the second law. Chapter 2 and method 7. Exercises: TD 2 Ex 2 (inclined plane), TD 2 Ex 3, TD 3 Ex 2.
  4. Energy. Chapter 3 and method 8. Exercises: TD 4 Ex 1, Ex 4 and Ex 5.
  5. Polar coordinates (not sure they are in midterm 1: TD 5). Chapter 4 and method 9. Exercises: TD 5 Ex 2, then Ex 2 and Ex 4 of the November 2021 midterm.
  6. The mock exam. The October 2022 paper in 1 h, with no notes and no calculator, then the solution. Reread the summary and the traps.
