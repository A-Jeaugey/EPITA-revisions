The instructions of the papers

  • Answer on the worksheet.2023, 2024, 2025Answers go in the boxes of the paper: answer tables, Karnaugh grids already drawn.
  • Do not show any calculation unless you are explicitly asked.2023, 2024, 2025Simplifications and canonical forms: only the result counts. Do your calculations on scratch paper, then check them with a Karnaugh map.
  • The result must not contain parentheses.simplifying, since 2023The expected form is a sum of products, as short as possible (method 1).
  • No points will be given to an expression if its Karnaugh map is wrong.Karnaugh, since 2023The completed map (circles included) is graded before the expression: one misplaced 1 cancels everything. In 2025, the instruction also forbids EXCLUSIVE-OR in the expressions read from the map (method 3).
  • Do not use a pencil or red ink.2023, 2024, 2025Use a pen, as for the other subjects.
  • The maximum number of gates is given.NAND gates, 2025AND in 2 gates, OR in 3, XOR in 5 (method 5).

Fixed format since 2023

Before 2023, the end-of-S1 exam mixed number systems (B1) and Boolean algebra. Since then, it covers only Boolean algebra, in this order:

What the course does not say

Undefined cells (Φ) come up in 11 papers out of 13, but chapter 1 does not mention them: sheet 4 explains them with examples from the papers.
