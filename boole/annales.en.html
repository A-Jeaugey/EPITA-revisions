The 13 papers, exercise by exercise.

The Boolean algebra papers we found, from the 2014-2015 midterm to the 2025-2026 B2 exam, retakes included. Up to 2022-2023, the end-of-S1 exam began with number systems (the B1 syllabus): those exercises are greyed out. Evolution

The format, from one paper to the next

  1. 2014 → 2022The end-of-S1 exam (“S1 midterm”, 1 hr 30 min) begins with two number-system exercises, sometimes three: conversions, 8-bit operations, written operations. Then Boolean algebra, worth 10 to 14 points depending on the year: a circuit to simplify, expressions (simplify, canonical forms, EXCLUSIVE-OR), Karnaugh maps, a problem (code converter, comparator, adder).
  2. 2023-2024A break: “Exam 2”, 1 hr 30 min, entirely on Boolean algebra, always in the same order. Simplify without parentheses, canonical forms, Karnaugh maps with Φ, a short problem, a one-bit comparator.
  3. 2024-2025Same layout, in four exercises: the problem (two tanks, three lights) has its impossible cases as Φ.
  4. 2025-2026“B2 exam”, same layout in five exercises: the last one asks for AND, OR and EXCLUSIVE-OR with NAND gates, with the number of gates imposed. Three of the four expressions to simplify are the ones from tutorial 1.
  5. RetakesThose of 2016-2017 and 2017-2018 (45 min, in English): number systems, expressions and a Karnaugh map; the 2017-2018 one reuses the circuit from the course.
Reused

Some exercises come back unchanged, and have only one card in the practice page: the circuit from the course to simplify (2014-2015, 2019-2020, 2022-2023 and the 2017-2018 retake), the comparator of two 2-bit numbers and the six outputs (2014-2015 and 2019-2020), the Aiken code converter (2016-2017 and 2020-2021). The Karnaugh maps of 2021-2022 come back in 2023-2024, the “4 × N in BCD” table of 2017-2018 in 2022-2023. And tutorial 1: its expression S6 was in the 2016-2017 midterm, S2, S5 and S7 in the 2025-2026 exam.
