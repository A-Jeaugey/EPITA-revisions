Rules of the papers

What the header of the paper says

Copied from the 9 papers found (2017 to 2024), in the words of the official English versions where they exist. These rules count in the grade: read them now, not on the day.

Caml:
  • You must answer on this subject.7 papers out of 9In the space provided, in pen: no pencil, no red pen (since 2021). In 2017 and April 2019, there were separate answer sheets.
  • All Caml code not indented will not be marked.9 papers out of 9then and else under the if, one match case per line, the | aligned. The presentation is marked.
  • In the absence of any indication in the document, the only functions that you can use are failwith and invalid_arg (no other predefined function of Caml).9 papers out of 9No List.length, no abs, no max: rewrite what you need. Operators (+, mod, =, &&, ::…) are still allowed. No official answer key uses @: avoid it.
  • Any Caml code must be followed by the result of its evaluation: the Caml answer.8 papers out of 9Under each function: val insert_mult : int -> 'a -> 'a list -> 'a list = <fun>. Missing only in 2018; since 2023 the (French) paper adds, in bold, that it “is part of the mark”.
  • Only one version must be given for each function to write.2023 and 2024Translated from the French papers. In 2024 also: follow the application examples given, document your own functions, and the last function written is the one that answers the question.

The closest format

Since 2023, Caml has its own exam, out of 20: three exercises worth 4 to 9 points. The October 2024 paper (1 h 30: a list of lists, an insertion sort with an order function, a Mystery) is the best mock exam.

Never on its own

Recursion on integers (factorial, gcd, power) has come up in no paper: it is there to learn the reasoning, which every list exercise reuses. Strings and printing have never come up either.

Organisation

A plan in six sessions

About 1 h 30 per session, in order. Add ten minutes of MCQ every day.

  1. The language and the Caml answer. Chapter 1, above all parts 5, 6 and 8, then method 4. Exercises: seminar 3.5 and 3.7.
  2. Reasoning recursively. Chapter 2 (parts 1 to 4 and 7) and method 1. Exercises: lists tutorial 1.4 and 1.5.
  3. Building a list, two exceptions. Chapter 3, parts 1 to 5, and methods 2 and 3. Exercises: insert_nth, remove_duplicates, remove_nth.
  4. Sorted lists, two lists, pairs. Chapter 3, parts 6 to 8. Exercises: delete, assoc, dominoes, alternate lists.
  5. Higher-order functions. Chapter 4 and method 6. Exercises: for_all2, how_many, insert_post.
  6. The mock exam. Method 5, then the October 2024 paper in 1 h 30, on paper, without the course. Mark it with the practice page, then reread the summary sheet and the traps.
