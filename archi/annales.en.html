The 10 papers, exercise by exercise.

The Archi papers found, from the 2016-2017 midterm to the B1 Examination of 2025-2026. The older ones contain exercises that are no longer on the syllabus: they are greyed out. Everything else has a step-by-step solution in the practice page, and every official answer has been recomputed: none is wrong.

Changes

The format, from one paper to the next

  1. 2016 → 20221 hr 30 min, five or six exercises. Besides the conversions and the written operations: powers of two to simplify, binary prefixes (Ki, Mi, Gi), addition in base 8, unknown bases or a memory map, which are not on this year’s syllabus. The conversions also used bases 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9 and 13: the course’s method applies to them, but no paper has asked for them since 2023.
  2. 2023-2024A break: four fixed exercises, always in the same order. Conversions between bases 2, 10 and 16, four written operations (including the multiplication), an 8-bit table, IEEE 754.
  3. 2024-2025Same plan. The hexadecimal addition has three terms, like the course’s example; the binary numbers to convert are written in groups of 4.
  4. 2025-2026Same plan in 1 hr instead of 1 hr 30 min, with fewer rows. The multiplication is exactly the one of 2023-2024.
Repeats

Some exercises come back word for word: the division of 2016-2017 in 2019-2020, the multiplication of 2023-2024 in 2025-2026, the four 8-bit calculations (250 + 10, 4 − 10, 120 + 10, −126 − 10) in 2019-2020 and 2022-2023, the conversion of 100110011.10011 to hexadecimal in 2020-2021 and 2021-2022.
