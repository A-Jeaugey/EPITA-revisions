What came up, exercise by exercise.

The analysis ECUE has changed its name almost every year (ASI in 2024, SR in 2025, APIS in 2026) but not its content. Two complete exams are the model for December; the course tests and the integral exercises of the older papers are the model for the 23 November course test. Each exercise links to its worked card.

What the two papers have in common
  • Six independent exercises, with the instruction “the rigour of your write-up will be taken into account in the mark”.
  • An adjacent sequences exercise that starts with “recall the definition”.
  • A Landau exercise with the instruction “stating all the possible comparisons and justifying”.
  • A course question to prove, taken from the “Proofs to know” sheet.
  • A recursive sequence: induction, monotonicity from the sign of \(u_{n+1} - u_n\), convergence, limit.
  • Three points on subsequences: \((u_{2n})\) and \((u_{2n+1})\), or two subsequences with different limits.
  • No documents or calculators; a penalty for untidy papers.
