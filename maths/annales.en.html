The mistakes in the official documents

Found by redoing every computation. The practice exercises give the right answer and say so.

Answer keys of the papers
  • 2019, ex. 5: anagrams of “tableau”: the answer key writes \(\frac{5!}{2!} = 60\). The word has 7 letters, two of them a: \(\frac{7!}{2!} = 2{,}520\).
  • 2019, ex. 7: the answer key divides by \(\P(A) = 0.7\) and finds \(\P_B(A) = \frac{3}{7}\). Divide by \(\P(B) = 0.05 + 0.1 + 0.05 + 0.2 = 0.4\): \(\P_B(A) = \frac{0.3}{0.4} = \frac{3}{4}\). The conclusion (not independent) is still right.
  • 2019, ex. 8: probability of being ill without being vaccinated: the answer key divides by 0.6 and finds about 15.6%. Right: \(\frac{0.94 \times 0.1}{0.4} = \frac{47}{200}\), that is 23.5%.
  • 2020, ex. 1: “every student has at least one classmate less than 5 min away” translated without \(e' \neq e\): the statement becomes always true (each student is 0 min away from themselves).
  • 2022, ex. 5 and DP 2023, ex. 6: “E(X)”, “V(X)” written for another variable (\(Y\), \(X_k\)): typos with no effect on the numbers.
  • DP 2024, ex. 6.2: “\(k \in \ent{1}{n}\)” in the paper: read \(\ent{1}{20}\). Ex. 1.4: \(\binom{7}{1}\binom{24}{3}\) only chooses 4 cards out of 5: the situation must impose one card.
Lectures, summary sheets and tutorials
  • Seminar 4.27.3 (FR and EN): “\(a \mathrel{\mathcal{R}} b \Rightarrow a \mathrel{\mathcal{R}} b\)” is a tautology: read \(a \mathrel{\mathcal{R}} b \Rightarrow b \mathrel{\mathcal{R}} a\) (symmetry).
  • Summary sheet “Conditional probability”: the last term of the law of total probability is written \(\P(A \cap B_n)\P(B_n)\): read \(\P(A \mid B_n)\P(B_n)\).
  • Summary sheet “Counting”: Pascal stated for \(k \in \ent{0}{n}\); \(\binom{n}{k-1}\) only makes sense for \(1 \leq k \leq n\) (or with \(\binom{n}{-1} = 0\)).
  • Basic functions handout: \(\ln(a^n) = n\ln(a)\) “for \(a \in \R\)”: \(a > 0\) is needed.
  • Theorems and proofs, proof 3: “Let \(A \in \mathcal{P}(\Omega)\)” without introducing \(B\): read “Let \((A, B) \in \mathcal{P}(\Omega)^2\)”.
  • Tutorial 2, ex. 5.19: the English sheet is about \(\ent{0}{n}\) (\(\E = \frac{n}{2}\), \(\V = \frac{n(n+2)}{12}\)), the French one about \(\{1, \dots, n\}\) (\(\E = \frac{n+1}{2}\), \(\V = \frac{n^2 - 1}{12}\)): each version has its answer.
