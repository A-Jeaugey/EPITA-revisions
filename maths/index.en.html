The instructions, and the marked write-up

The papers are booklets: each answer goes in its box, and space is limited. Instructions copied from the papers (in French on the papers).

  • “The rigour of your write-up will be taken into account in the mark.”4 papers out of 4A right answer without justification does not get all the points. What to write, exercise by exercise: the methods.
  • “A penalty of one point on the mark out of 20 will be applied to untidy papers.”4 papers out of 4One point out of 20 is 2 points on a scale of 40.
  • “No documents, no calculators.”4 papers out of 4The Moodle tests allowed a calculator; the exam does not. The papers ask for the result “as a simplified fraction”, “as a whole number” or “without doing the computation to the end”: read the requested form.
  • “No answer written in pencil will be marked.”4 papers out of 4Pencil on your rough paper if you like, pen on the answer booklet.
  • “The marking scheme is out of 40 points. The mark will be brought back to 20 by dividing by 2.”2024Out of 30 points in DP 2023 (“brought back to 20 by the rule of three”), out of 20 in LE 2023. The marks are given per exercise, never per question.
  • “Read the whole paper before starting. There are 6 exercises in all.”3 papers out of 4Six exercises in 2 hrs (DP 2023, DP 2024, OBM 2024): about 20 minutes each. Start with those whose plan you know (conditional probability, binomial distribution, proof from the lecture).
What the answer keys write · DP
  • One sentence per factor and the model named: “the order no longer matters”, “we go through the complement”.
  • Events defined by a letter and a sentence; “since \(\{S, \overline{S}\}\) is a partition of \(\Omega\)” before the law of total probability; the formula in letters, then the numbers, then the simplified fraction.
  • Random variables: \(X(\Omega)\), each probability, the name of the distribution; \(\E\) and \(\V\) in detail, “do not just drop the results”. Binomial: “sum of \(n\) independent Bernoulli variables with the same parameter”.

What the answer keys write · OBM
  • Definitions with their quantifiers and their sets: \(f^{-1}(B) = \{x \in E,\ f(x) \in B\}\).
  • A true or false is always justified: a proof, or a counterexample with numbers (“\(g(0) = g(2) = 1\) and \(0 \neq 2\)”).
  • A restriction comes with both sets and its reason (“strictly increasing on \([1, +\infty[\)”). For a relation: “After recalling the definition” (OBM 2024).

What we do not know
  • Complex numbers and integrals: the 2024 OBM exam had 16 points of them out of 40 (exercises 5 and 6). The 2026 seminar does not cover them and integrals belong to APIS: the site assumes they are not on the OBM syllabus.
  • Binary relations: in every OBM or combined paper since 2019, but no official 2026 document gives their definitions. Those on the site come from the 2022 and 2023 answer keys.
  • The 2024 papers have no answer key in the archive: their answers are recomputed. The 2025 paper is not available, except for an extract quoted in Tutorial 1.
