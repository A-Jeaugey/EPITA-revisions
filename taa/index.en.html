Past exams

What comes up

Based on the 9 papers found, from 2017 to 2024. Until 2022, the abstract types part opened the algo paper (4 to 6 points out of 20); since 2023, it has been a separate exam, out of 10.

5 papers out of 9

Write the preconditions and the axioms of a new operation on a recursive list: concatenate, ispresent, search, deletegreater… Every year since 2023.

4 papers out of 9

Course questions on the vocabulary: internal operation, observer, constant, partial or auxiliary operation, areas of the signature.

3 papers out of 9

Iterative list: the modify operation (2017, 2021), then a mystery operation to apply to a list (2024).

Never came up

Stacks, queues, sets. But they are on this year’s syllabus: the MiMos end on 25 October and are “assumed known” from the exam week on.

Organization

A plan in five sessions

About 1 h 30 per session. Do the sessions in order, and the MCQ every day.

  1. The vocabulary. Chapter 1 in full, then the course questions of method 7, to learn by heart.
  2. The recursive list. Chapter 2, parts 1 to 4, then method 3 and the “Recursive lists” exercises in practice.
  3. The iterative list. Chapter 2, parts 5 to 8, method 4, then 10 mystery operations with the generator.
  4. Stacks, queues, sets. Chapter 3 and 10 terms to reduce with the generator.
  5. The mock exam. The October 2024 paper in 30 minutes, without looking at the lessons, then the solution. Reread the summary sheet.
