Instructions

How to use them

1. Under exam conditions

Print or open the paper, with no calculator and no course notes. The papers give no duration: time yourself and write down your time.

2. Mark your work

Open the step-by-step solution of each exercise. Compare the method, not just the result: the intermediate diagrams count.

3. Track your mistakes

Mark the exercise “to review” if you got stuck. The paper’s MCQ enters your spaced repetition as soon as you answer it.
