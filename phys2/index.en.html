What has changed

These seven papers come from another version of the course. Statics (moments, equilibrium of a beam) came up in almost every one; this year’s complete course does not mention it. Conversely, oscillators and waves, in the course this year (chapters 3 to 5), appear in none of them: their models are TD 6 to 8 and the October 2025 midterm from Moodle (drop with friction, ISS).
