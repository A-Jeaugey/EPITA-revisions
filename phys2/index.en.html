Physics B2 · Circular motion

The point turns, the forces hold it.

The rest of point mechanics: locate a point in polar coordinates, describe a circular motion, then explain it with the second law and the energy of B1 (a ball on a sphere, a chute, a loop). This is the known syllabus of the end-of-semester exam; statics will be added when the course publishes it.

The best models

January 2020 and June 2018: an exercise on the second law and energy on an arc of a circle (chute, loop), the core of chapter 2. January 2022: 100 % polar coordinates and circular motion, the only paper without statics.

Not in the course yet

Statics (moments, rotational and translational equilibrium) comes up in almost every paper, for 5 to 7 points. It is not in the published course: the exercises are listed in the past exams and will be solved when the chapter arrives.
