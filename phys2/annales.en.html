Not in the course yet

Statics (moment of a force, rotational and translational equilibrium of a beam, a ladder, a sign) comes up in 6 papers out of 7, often for 5 to 7 points. It will be added when the B2 course publishes it.

Frenet basis

The papers ask their circular-motion questions “in the Frenet basis”. On a circle, this is the polar basis of the course: the translation is in chapter 2, part 6.
